Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 19, 2026

Lockstep is designed to generate authenticator codes locally and synchronize only encrypted vault records. This policy explains what data is handled, why it is needed, and the choices available to you.

Information Lockstep handles

  • Google account information: your stable Google account identifier, email address, and display name are used to sign you in, identify your vault, and display your welcome message.
  • Authenticator information: issuer, account label, TOTP parameters, encrypted secret ciphertext, encryption IV, key version, and synchronization metadata are stored to provide your authenticator vault.
  • Security information: Lockstep stores a salted App Lock verifier and an encrypted, wrapped vault key. Your App Lock passkey, unwrapped vault master key, recovery key, plaintext TOTP secret, and generated verification codes are not stored as readable server data.
  • Local preferences: theme, device-local account order and pins, lock timing, encrypted offline vault data, and pending synchronization operations may be stored on your device.
  • User-selected website content: when you explicitly choose Scan QR code, the extension captures the currently visible tab and lets you select a region. The screenshot and QR decoding are processed locally for that action and are not uploaded to Lockstep.
  • Operational information: the hosting platform may process ordinary request metadata such as time, IP address, response status, and technical error information for security, reliability, and abuse prevention.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate you, provide and secure the authenticator vault, synchronize encrypted records between your Lockstep clients, restore pending offline changes, respond to support or security issues, and maintain service reliability. Lockstep does not use your data for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, or unrelated purposes.

Encryption and local processing

TOTP secrets are encrypted in the client with AES-256-GCM before synchronization. Verification codes, QR decoding, QR generation, backup creation, and backup import occur locally. The server receives encrypted secret ciphertext and the metadata required to synchronize it, but it does not receive the vault master key required to decrypt that ciphertext. Anyone holding a recovery key or plaintext backup can access the associated authenticator secrets, so those exports must be protected.

Sharing and service providers

Lockstep does not sell personal or sensitive user data. Data is processed only as needed through Google OAuth for sign-in, Google Cloud Firestore for encrypted vault persistence, and the infrastructure provider hosting the Lockstep API and web app. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to address fraud or security abuse, or as part of a business transfer with the notices or consent required by applicable law.

Retention and deletion

Encrypted synchronized vault data and account metadata are retained while your Lockstep account exists. Device-local information remains until you sign out, reset the vault, clear the extension or browser storage, uninstall the extension, or remove the PWA data, depending on the data type. Use Lockstep’s reset-vault option to delete synchronized vault data. Backups and recovery keys downloaded to your device must be deleted by you separately. Infrastructure logs may be retained for the period configured by the hosting provider for security and reliability.

Your choices

You may choose not to scan website QR codes and instead use manual entry, local QR images, or standard otpauth:// URLs. You may sign out, clear local browser data, uninstall the extension, or reset the synchronized vault. Because Lockstep does not hold your unencrypted vault master key, losing all provisioned devices and recovery keys can make encrypted records permanently unrecoverable.

Children

Lockstep is not directed to children under the minimum age required to manage their own Google account in their jurisdiction, and it does not knowingly collect data from children for advertising or profiling.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when Lockstep’s features, providers, or legal obligations change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above and, when required, by providing additional notice.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion assistance, contact the developer through the support contact shown on the Lockstep Chrome Web Store listing or the distribution page from which you obtained Lockstep.

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
